HomeFIT — room-aware product discovery

Solo product

Upload a room photo and HomeFIT extracts its visual style, dominant palette, and detected furniture, then retrieves and reranks a merchant catalog by visual fit, color harmony, and room-profile alignment. A conversational agent handles refinement in natural language ("show cheaper ones", "find lamps for this room"). Built solo end-to-end with a hand-labeled evaluation benchmark across 39 room types against a 1,224-product catalog.
